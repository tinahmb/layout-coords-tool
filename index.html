Drag this button to your bookmarks bar.
Later, click it on any page you're working on.
  1. Drag the button above to your browser's bookmarks bar (not click — drag).
  2. Open your own site or project (localhost, staging, wherever you're working).
  3. Click the bookmark. A small panel appears on your page.
  4. Drag any button or image to where you want it, drop it, and copy the CSS shown.
  5. Paste that CSS into your code, or hand it to your AI coding agent.
  6. Click "Remove tool" when done — nothing is left behind.

Prefer the console?

If you're already in DevTools, paste this into the Console on your own page instead:

var s = document.createElement('script');
s.src = 'https://layout-coords-tool.pages.dev/overlay.js';
document.body.appendChild(s);
⚠️ Nothing is saved or installed. The tool only exists in the current browser tab. Refresh the page, or click "Remove tool," and it's completely gone — your project files are never touched.
